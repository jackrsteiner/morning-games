## How to play

Get on all fours and balance a cushion “shell” on each turtle's back. One turtle leads a slow trail around the room while the other follows: curve around furniture, change direction, and crawl through open doorways. Swap leaders whenever someone calls, **“Trail turn!”**

At any moment, either player can call, **“Crow!”** Both turtles stop, lower toward the floor, and tuck their arms and legs underneath themselves to hide under their shells. Stay hidden for one slow breath, call **“All clear!”**, and continue the trek.

If a shell slips, freeze, replace it, and continue from the same spot. Keep it cooperative - there is no race.
