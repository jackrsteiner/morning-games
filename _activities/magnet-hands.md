## How to play

Stand facing each other about an arm's length apart. Pair your right palm with your partner's left and your left with their right. Keep each pair 10-20 cm apart without touching.

One person slowly “pulls” the magnets up, down, sideways, across the body, toward the floor, or overhead. Both players keep moving to preserve the gap: step, crouch, rise, turn, or briefly balance on one foot.

Change leaders by saying, **“Your magnets are stronger now.”** If a connection breaks, freeze, realign your palms, and continue.
