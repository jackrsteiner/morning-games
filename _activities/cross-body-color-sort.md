## Set up

Place the red cup on the left and the green cup on the right. Arrange the loose pieces in two mixed or winding trails between the player and the cups.

## How to play

Assign the **right hand to red** and the **left hand to green**. Pick up one piece of each colour at the same time. Bring both hands toward the cups, cross the right hand over to the red cup and the left hand over to the green cup, then release both pieces together.

Uncross, return to the trails, and collect the next pair. Keep a steady rhythm: **pick, cross, drop, open**. When the pieces are sorted, tip them out, switch the cups' positions and hand assignments, and begin again.

If only one colour remains, use one hand while the other makes the same empty journey. Large paper shapes work well when small building pieces are not appropriate.
