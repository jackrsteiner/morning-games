## Set up

Stand side by side on a non-slip floor with a clear space around you. Put the cushion nearby as the stork nest.

## How to play

Become storks. Lift one foot, rest it against the standing leg’s calf or knee, and spread your arms like wings.

One stork is the caller and stays on one leg too. The caller gives stork moves to copy while balancing: **flap slowly**, **look left**, **look right**, **wings up high**, **peck down low**, or **stretch your neck tall**. Sometimes the caller says “**Go to the nest!**” and everyone may put a foot down and step to the cushion, then return and balance again.

If a foot touches down early, laugh, take a breath, and lift it again. Swap callers often, and switch legs each round.

## Make it easier

Lightly hold a wall, chair back, or each other’s hand, and keep the lifted foot just off the floor.

## Make it harder

Stand on a folded towel, balance with eyes closed for a slow count of three, or add a trick call such as “Flap—only with one wing!”
