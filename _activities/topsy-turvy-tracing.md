## Set up

Print the tracing sheet and place it flat. Sit or stand centered between the two patterns.

## How to play

Place one index finger on each green dot, then trace both lines at the same time.

- **Row 1:** Start in the middle and move outward.
- **Row 2:** Start at the outside edges and move inward.
- **Row 3:** Start one finger in the spiral's center and the other at its outside end.
- **Row 4:** Start both fingers on the left and move right.

Move slowly and keep both fingers on their lines. At the end of a row, lift your fingers, move to the next pair of dots, and continue. When you reach the bottom, return to the top.
