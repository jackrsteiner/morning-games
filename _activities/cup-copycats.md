## Set up

Sit facing each other. Each player holds one capped marker upright in each hand and rests an upside-down paper cup over the top of one marker. Keep the markers below shoulder height and away from faces.

## How to play

The leader slowly moves the **cup wand** up, down, sideways, in a circle, or across the body. The other player copies the route while keeping their own cup balanced.

Whenever the leader says **“Switch!”**, both players bring the bare marker up inside the cup, slide the first marker out, and continue with the cup balanced on the other hand's marker. Change leaders after several paths.

If a cup falls, both players freeze while it is reset, then continue from the place where it dropped. Use smooth paths rather than flicking or tossing the cups.
