## Set up

Sit together at a table. Scatter the capped markers within easy reach and place the open container in the middle. Keep the whole game seated; use plastic rather than glass.

## How to play

One player closes their eyes or chooses to wear the sleep mask. Using touch alone, find one marker, work out which end is pointing forward, and guide it into the container. The other player may give quiet **warmer**, **colder**, **left**, and **right** clues, but does not move the marker or container.

Continue until every marker is inside, then tip them out and swap roles. If the opening is difficult to find, the clue-giver can tap the side of the container to make a sound target.

Looking is always allowed. The challenge is careful, non-visual movement—not keeping the mask on.
