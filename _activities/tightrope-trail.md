## Set up

Stick a straight line of tape along the floor, about three to four big steps long, with clear space on both sides.

## How to play

Become tightrope walkers. Stretch your arms out wide and walk the line heel to toe, placing each front heel against the back toes.

The partner off the line is the ringmaster and calls one move at a time: **walk forwards**, **walk backwards**, **freeze**, **crouch low**, **turn around**, or **tiptoe**. Hold each move steady before the next call. Stepping off is fine: step back on where you left and continue.

Swap roles at the end of the line so everyone walks and everyone calls.

## Make it easier

Use a wider strip, such as a long scarf or two tape lines side by side, and let the ringmaster hold a hand.

## Make it harder

Walk with eyes looking at a point ahead instead of at your feet, carry a soft toy on one flat hand, or bend the tape into a zigzag.
